# Catalyst-dependent Biginelli synthesis

**Status:** Completed four-person CH214 laboratory capstone, Spring 2026.

## Question and contribution

The team compared catalyst conditions for synthesis and purification of a dihydropyrimidinone derivative. I was responsible for the zinc(II) chloride condition: reaction setup and monitoring, product recovery, successive recrystallization, and interpretation of IR and NMR data.

## Results and interpretation

The recorded recovered mass fell from 0.0867 g after the first purification to 0.0560 g after the second. That change is consistent with impurity removal and/or material loss; mass alone cannot establish identity or purity. The team report includes spectral evidence, but this short case study does not make a quantitative yield or purity claim.

**Evidence basis:** CH214 capstone report (Mukherjee, Nalepka, Moolchandani, and Nguyen, Spring 2026) as summarized in my 2026 engineering portfolio. This was a shared laboratory project.
