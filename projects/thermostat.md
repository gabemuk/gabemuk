# Custom thermostat

**Status:** Working electromechanical course prototype, Spring 2025.

## Objective and build

I built a physical thermostat prototype combining an Arduino, sensors, LCD, switches, indicators, wiring, and a CAD-designed enclosure. The enclosure used 3D-printed and laser-cut parts. The work involved soldering, mechanical fit, electrical integration, and debugging during assembly.

## Validation and limits

The assembled device was powered and passed functional checks during the project. My engineering portfolio includes photographs of bench wiring, enclosure integration, and the finished hardware. I do not have a published control-accuracy, response-time, or long-duration reliability study, so I make no performance claim beyond a working prototype.

**Evidence basis:** Build photographs and description in my 2026 engineering portfolio. Firmware, CAD source, and a reproducible bill of materials are not published.
